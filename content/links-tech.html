Papers — Download & Read These

PaperLinkWhy It Matters
Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al., 2017)arxiv.org PDFThe Transformer paper. Invented the architecture behind every major AI system today. 8 pages. Read this.
Large Language Models: A Survey (Minaee et al., 2024)arxiv.org PDFComprehensive 100+ page survey of the LLM landscape — architectures, training, benchmarks, capabilities, limitations.
GPT-3: Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (2020)arxiv.org PDFThe paper that showed scale creates emergent capabilities. 75 pages. Seminal moment in AI history.
Constitutional AI (Anthropic, 2022)arxiv.org PDFAnthropic's technique for harmlessness via self-critique. How Claude is trained.
Scaling Laws for Neural Language Models (2020)arxiv.org PDFOpenAI's discovery that LLM performance scales predictably with compute, data, and parameters. The scientific basis for the AI arms race.
Chain-of-Thought Prompting (Wei et al., 2022)arxiv.org PDFShowed that asking models to "think step by step" dramatically improves reasoning. Why CoT prompting works.
Sparks of AGI (Microsoft, 2023)arxiv.org PDFMicrosoft researchers' early evaluation of GPT-4. Argued it shows early signs of general intelligence. Controversial and fascinating.
