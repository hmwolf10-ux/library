Alt, M, A, ARemove all tracer arrows from sheet
Ctrl + Shift + {Select ALL precedent cells (not just direct — goes all levels deep)
Ctrl + Shift + }Select ALL dependent cells (all levels)
Alt, M, VOpen Evaluate Formula tool — step through formula calculation one piece at a time
F9 (inside formula bar)Evaluate selected part of a formula — highlight a section and press F9 to see its result
EscapeExit formula bar without saving changes (critical for undoing accidental edits)
Ctrl + Shift + UExpand or collapse the formula bar
Alt, M, KError Checking — steps through formula errors in the sheet
Ctrl + F3Open Name Manager — see, edit, and delete all named ranges
Ctrl + Shift + F3Create named ranges from row or column header labels automatically
F3 (inside formula)Paste a named range into a formula — browse all names
